The reliable way to build how much growth is realistic in a season is short, frequent practice rather than weekend marathons. When your student misses a question, the question is not finished — the explanation is the assignment.

The second is avoidance: students practice what they like and skip what they need, so let the diagnostic, not preference, set the menu.
