Start with the free diagnostic

Every GradeHoney pack begins the same way: a free diagnostic that sorts your student by level before a single lesson is assigned. Parents told us their biggest problem was not knowing where to start — the diagnostic is the answer.

How it works

  • Your student takes a short, untimed-but-recorded diagnostic for the test they are preparing for.
  • Every answer maps to a skill on that test's published scope and sequence.
  • The family dashboard places your student at the first unmastered module in each strand — and shows you the same map.
  • That first diagnostic becomes the baseline for the GradeHoney improvement promise: we measure growth against your student's own starting point, never against a named score.
